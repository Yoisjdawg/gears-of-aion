# Daevas of E-Day: the next 100 (Gears world, AION 2 combat)

Direction from the creator: the world should look like Gears of War (the Siege / Horde open world), the Gears guns are off for now, and the combat goes all-in on AION 2.

Done so far: the Siege Downtown streets from your install, 8 classes with 8 skills each, chains, crowd control, cast bars, target lock, evasion, flight, spirits, traps, stealth, the AION basic attack, Locust E-holes and pathfinding.

**Title:** Gears of Aion

**Short description:** AION 2's Daevas land in Gears of War: E-Day's Siege. Eight AION classes as Gears heroes, chain skills, Daeva wings and four Locust bosses, in the city from your own install.

**Description:**

The rift over Sera opened, and the Daevas came through. Pick one of eight AION-style classes, each played as a Gears of War: E-Day hero, and hold the Siege district against the Locust.

- Three Siege districts read from your Gears of War: E-Day install, under a living sky: time of day, rain, dust storms, thunderstorms and night.
- AION combat: eight skills per class with chains, elements, crowd control, Stigmas, an ultimate with Daeva Transformation, and Daeva flight.
- The Locust come out of E-holes: Wretches, Drones, Boomers, Tickers, Reavers, snipers, grenadiers, Spore Matrons, Seeders, and the bosses Vrak, the Corpser, Vrak the Scorched and the Brumak.
- An eight-wave campaign, plus Endless, Siege Defence, Boss Rush, Time Attack, Daily Challenge, Custom Waves, the Practice Range and a Tutorial.
- AION loot: grades to Mythic, enchanting to +15, manastones and sets, crafting, missions, achievements, titles and Abyss ranks.
- Gears music, voices and sounds, with captions; full key and gamepad rebinding; accessibility options.

Needs Gears of War: E-Day (PC) and AION 2 (Steam) installed. Neither game is modified or started, and nothing from either game is in the download. Solo.
